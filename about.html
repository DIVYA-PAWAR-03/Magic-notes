How It Works

Magic Notes is a client-side only application — no server, no database, no sign-up required. All your notes are saved in your browser's localStorage, which means they persist across sessions on the same browser and device. Your data is completely private and never leaves your machine. Simply open index.html in any modern browser and start taking notes immediately.
